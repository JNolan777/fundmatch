Why Now

Three forces converging
right now.

FundMatch wouldn't have been possible 3 years ago. Today, all the ingredients are ready.

🤖

Conversational AI is mature

Large language models (Claude, GPT-4) can now hold nuanced conversations, extract structured data, and write compelling grant proposals — at production quality. This wasn't true in 2021.

🗃️

Foundation data is accessible

The Dutch ANBI registry (Belastingdienst) is public and machine-readable. 43,000+ foundations with sector, geography, and financial data — ready to be matched against at scale.

📈

Sector demand is growing

NL nonprofit sector spend grew 11% in 2024. More organisations need more funding, with smaller teams. The pressure to do more with less is creating urgency for better tools.

⚡ The window is open right now — before a well-funded competitor builds this for Europe.

The Moat

The more it's used,
the smarter it gets.

FundMatch learns continuously from every interaction — building a competitive advantage no one can copy from a cold start.

💬

User describes their project

Every onboarding adds structured data: org type, sector, budget range, geography, cause area.

↓
📈

Model improves for everyone

Anonymised learnings feed back — every new user benefits from all previous users. Data stays private.

↓
🎯

AI suggests matches

The model ranks foundations by fit. Users accept, reject, or refine — each signal trains the next match.

↑
✍️

Proposals win or lose

Awarded grants teach the AI what language and framing works for which foundation. Rejections teach what doesn't.

Con: Free tier has support cost. Slower revenue ramp.
Option B — Paid-only with Trial

14-day free trial → Subscribe

€79/mo
Solo — 1 user, unlimited matching, 10 proposals/month, basic reporting.
€199/mo
Team — 5 users, unlimited proposals, full reporting + CRM, API access.
Custom
Enterprise / consultancies — multi-org, white-label, dedicated support.
Pro: Cleaner unit economics, faster revenue.
Con: Higher acquisition friction, needs outbound sales.
Recommendation: Start with Freemium for NL market penetration. Switch to paid-only once brand is established or when expanding to the agency/enterprise segment.
